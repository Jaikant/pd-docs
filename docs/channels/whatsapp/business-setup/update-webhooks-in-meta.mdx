# Update Webhooks in Meta
**Estimated Time: 3 minutes**

After adding the Phone Number ID in the Predictable Dialogs app, the next screen gives you the webhook URL and webhook token. Copy them one at a time and add them to the **Configure Webhooks** section in **Step 2. Production setup** in the Meta Console.

The **Configure Webhooks** section is just before the **Register your WhatsApp phone number** section completed in the previous page.

## Add the webhook details
- In the Meta Console, go to **Basic setup** and click **Step 2. Production setup**.
- Open **Configure Webhooks**.
- Copy the webhook URL from Predictable Dialogs and paste it into **Callback URL**.
- Copy the webhook token from Predictable Dialogs and paste it into **Verify token**.
- Click **Verify and save** to save the webhook information.

![Update Webhooks Image](/img/whatsapp/update-webhooks-in-meta.webp)

## Subscribe to messages
- After saving, return to **Step 2. Production setup** and open **Configure Webhooks** again.
- A new list called **Webhook fields** appears below this section.
- Search for the field called **messages**.
- Ensure the toggle at the end of the **messages** row is set to **Subscribed**. If it is set to **Unsubscribed**, click it to make it **Subscribed**.
